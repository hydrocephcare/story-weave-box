(ii) 2 ADP + 2 Pi + 2 NAD⁺ + 2 NADH + 2 H⁺ + 4 ADP $\longrightarrow$ 4 ATP + 2 H₂O + 2 NADH + 2 H⁺ + 2 NAD⁺ + 2 ADP

(c) Explain why the two equations (a iii and b iii, above) are different.

In aerobic glycolysis, the two molecules of NADH made in the cytosol are reoxidized to NAD⁺ by transfer of their electrons to the ETC in the mitochondria. The electrons are used to reduce O₂ to water $2\text{NADH} + 2\text{H}^+ + \text{O}_2 \longrightarrow 2\text{NAD}^+ + 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$

In anaerobic glycolysis, the two NADH are reused by the LDH to generate $2\text{NAD}^+$ that are reused by the G3P-DH to keep glycolysis going.
