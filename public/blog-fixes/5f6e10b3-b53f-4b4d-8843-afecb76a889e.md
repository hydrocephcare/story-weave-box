## Strategies for improving communication among doctors (20 Marks)
*(8 strategies, 2.5 marks each)*

- **Regular Team Meetings**Encourage routine interdisciplinary meetings to discuss patient care.
- Facilitates collaboration and ensures consistent care.

- **Use of Standardized Communication Tools (SBAR)**Implement structured tools like SBAR (Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation) for verbal and written handoffs.
- Ensures clear, concise, and standardized communication, reducing the risk of miscommunication.

- **Active Listening and Mutual Respect**Promote active listening where doctors attentively process colleagues' input.
- Establish respect for differing opinions, fostering a supportive, non-judgmental environment.

- **Clear and Accurate Documentation (EHR)**Maintain accurate, comprehensive electronic health records accessible to all healthcare providers.
- Reduces errors, improves efficiency, and ensures that all team members are informed of treatment plans.

- **Promoting a Culture of Open Feedback**Encourage giving and receiving constructive feedback in a non-threatening manner.
- Allows doctors to refine their communication, improving team performance and patient care.

- **Formal and Informal Case Discussions**Conduct formal case reviews (e.g., grand rounds) and informal discussions to share clinical experiences.
- Enhances collective knowledge and helps doctors make better clinical decisions.

- **Crisis Communication Training**Provide doctors with training in crisis communication techniques for high-stress situations.
- Helps teams to respond effectively under pressure and improve patient safety.

- **Utilizing Multidisciplinary Rounds**Hold multidisciplinary rounds with doctors, nurses, and allied health professionals.
- Ensures a holistic approach to patient care and improves coordination among healthcare providers.

## Effects of the placebo effect (10 Marks)
- **Symptom Improvement**Reduction in symptoms like pain, even without active treatment.

- **Psychological Benefits**Increased confidence and better mental well-being.

- **Reduction in Medication Use**Less need for actual medications, reducing side effects.

- **Altered Brain Chemistry**Brain releases endorphins or other chemicals, mimicking real treatment effects.

- **Temporary Effect**Relief is often short-lived and does not address the root cause.
