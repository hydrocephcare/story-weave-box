# Safe Motherhood Initiative, Birth Preparedness and Complication Readiness

**Year 4 · Obstetrics and Gynaecology · Maternal health and public health**

> **In one line:** the **Safe Motherhood Initiative** (1987) aims to reduce maternal deaths through family planning, antenatal care, clean delivery, emergency obstetric care and postnatal care; **birth preparedness and complication readiness** tackles the **three delays** (deciding, reaching, receiving care) that turn obstetric complications into deaths.

## What you must know

- **Maternal death**: death during pregnancy or within **42 days** of its end, from causes related to or aggravated by pregnancy or its management (not accidental or incidental).
- **MMR** = maternal deaths per 100,000 live births. Kenya about **350-530**; SDG target **below 70 by 2030**.
- Leading causes: **haemorrhage, hypertensive disorders, sepsis, unsafe abortion, obstructed labour**, plus indirect causes (malaria, HIV, anaemia).
- Safe Motherhood launched in **Nairobi in 1987** to halve maternal deaths by 2000.
- **Three delays**: deciding to seek care, reaching a facility, receiving adequate care.
- **Basic EmONC has seven signal functions**; comprehensive adds caesarean section and blood transfusion.
- BP/CR plan made with every woman by about **36 weeks**.

## 1. Maternal mortality

- **Maternal death** (WHO): the death of a woman while pregnant or within 42 days of the end of pregnancy, irrespective of duration or site, from any cause related to or aggravated by the pregnancy or its management, but not from accidental or incidental causes.
- **Maternal mortality ratio (MMR)** = maternal deaths per 100,000 live births. Kenya's MMR is about 350-530 per 100,000, far above the SDG target of below 70 by 2030.

> **Check:** the denominator is **live births** (a ratio), not total population; do not confuse MMR with the maternal mortality rate.

### Causes

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Direct** | Obstetric **haemorrhage** (antepartum and postpartum), **hypertensive disorders** (pre-eclampsia, eclampsia), **sepsis**, **unsafe abortion**, **obstructed labour** |
| **Indirect** | Malaria, HIV, anaemia, cardiac disease |

## 2. The Safe Motherhood Initiative

Launched in **Nairobi in 1987** to cut maternal deaths by half by 2000. Pillars:

1. **Family planning** (prevents high-risk and unwanted pregnancies).
2. **Antenatal care.**
3. **Clean and safe delivery** (skilled birth attendant).
4. **Essential obstetric care** (basic and comprehensive emergency obstetric and newborn care, EmONC).
5. **Postnatal care** and **safe abortion care** where legal.

### Signal functions of EmONC

| Level | Functions |
|---|---|
| **Basic EmONC (seven)** | Parenteral **antibiotics**; parenteral **oxytocic drugs**; parenteral **anticonvulsants** (magnesium sulphate); **manual removal of the placenta**; **removal of retained products**; **assisted vaginal delivery**; **neonatal resuscitation** |
| **Comprehensive EmONC** | All of the above **plus caesarean section and blood transfusion** |

> **Exam tip:** caesarean section and blood transfusion are the two extra functions that separate comprehensive from basic EmONC.

## 3. The three delays

| Delay | Examples |
|---|---|
| **1. Deciding to seek care** | Poor knowledge of danger signs, cultural beliefs, cost, a woman who cannot decide alone |
| **2. Reaching a facility** | Distance, poor roads, no transport or money |
| **3. Receiving adequate care** | Shortages of staff, blood, drugs, delay in triage, poor referral |

Birth preparedness addresses the first two delays; facility quality addresses the third.

## 4. Birth preparedness and complication readiness (BP/CR)

A plan made with every woman, her partner and family by about **36 weeks**:

- Choose the **place of birth** and the **skilled attendant**.
- Save money; arrange **transport** (and an alternative).
- Identify a **blood donor** and a decision-maker/companion.
- Pack essentials (maternity clothes, newborn items, health records).
- Know the **danger signs** and where to go.
- Register for insurance (for example the national health scheme).

### Danger signs to teach

Vaginal bleeding, severe headache with blurred vision, convulsions, fever, severe abdominal pain, reduced foetal movements, leaking of liquor, prolonged labour, and a retained placenta.

## 5. Respectful maternity care

Respect, privacy, informed consent, a **companion of choice** and freedom from abuse improve uptake of facility births. Disrespect and abuse is a recognised reason women avoid health facilities.

## Key points to revise

1. Maternal death: within 42 days of the end of pregnancy, related to or aggravated by pregnancy.
2. MMR per 100,000 live births; Kenya about 350-530; SDG target below 70.
3. Direct causes: haemorrhage, hypertensive disorders, sepsis, unsafe abortion, obstructed labour.
4. Safe Motherhood launched 1987 (Nairobi); five pillars including family planning and EmONC.
5. Three delays: decide, reach, receive.
6. Basic EmONC has seven signal functions; comprehensive adds caesarean and transfusion.
7. BP/CR plan by 36 weeks: place, attendant, transport, money, blood donor, danger signs.
