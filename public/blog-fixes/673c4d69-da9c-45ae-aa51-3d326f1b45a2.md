## Overview

**2ND SEMESTER**

## Assessment

Two continuous assessment tests will be given. Both will contribute **15%** of the total marks. Two assignments will be given and will contribute **5%** of the total marks.  

At least three practicals will be given and will contribute **10%** of the total marks. End of Semester Examination will contribute **70%** of the total marks.
