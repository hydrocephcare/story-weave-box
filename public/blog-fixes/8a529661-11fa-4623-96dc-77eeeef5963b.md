## Behavioural Sciences Quick-Fire Revision MCQs — Sets 1 & 2 (Past Paper)

*Two short revision MCQ sets compiled by Jitu. Questions only — no answer key.*

## Set 1

**1.** The group in an experiment which receives no treatment is called:

a. Control group
b. Experimental group
c. No group

**Answer: a) Control group**
Explanation: The control group receives no treatment.

**2.** The portion of your nervous system which controls breathing and digestion is:

a. Axon
b. Autonomic
c. Linear circuit
d. None of these

**Answer: b) Autonomic**
Explanation: The autonomic nervous system controls breathing and digestion.

**3.** The color, smell and feeling of the flowers are relayed through what part of brain:

a. Acetylcholine
b. Thalamus
c. Motor area
d. None of these

**Answer: b) Thalamus**
Explanation: The thalamus relays sensory information to the cortex.

**4.** A hungry person would find food to be a:

a. Primary reinforcer
b. Secondary reinforcer
c. None of these

**Answer: a) Primary reinforcer**
Explanation: Food satisfies a biological need, so it is a primary reinforcer.

**5.** A psychologist explains you that learning can be best defined in terms of underlying thought process. What theory is being stated:

a. Social learning
b. Cognitive learning
c. Trial and error
d. None of these

**Answer: b) Cognitive learning**
Explanation: Cognitive learning explains learning through underlying thought processes.

**6.** According to Maslow, a person with no job, no friends and no house can be self actualized:

a. 100 percent yes
b. 100 percent no
c. None of these

**Answer: b) 100 percent no**
Explanation: Maslow's hierarchy needs basic needs to be met before self-actualisation.

**7.** A need to establish and maintain relationship with other people:

a. Achievement motivation
b. Need for affiliation
c. None of these

**Answer: b) Need for affiliation**
Explanation: The need to form relationships is the need for affiliation.

**8.** Term n-ach was introduced by:

a. McCleland
b. Jung
c. Maslow
d. None of these

**Answer: a) McCleland**
Explanation: McClelland introduced the term n-Ach (need for achievement).

**9.** Repression refers to:

a. Primary defense mechanism
b. Regression
c. Frustration
d. None of these

**Answer: a) Primary defense mechanism**
Explanation: Repression is a primary defence mechanism.

**10.** Enduring dimensions of personality characteristics differentiating people from another is called:

a. Factor analysis
b. Trait
c. Determinism
d. None of these

**Answer: b) Trait**
Explanation: A trait is an enduring dimension of personality.

**11.** The theory which suggests that people learn attitude by observing their own behavior is:

a. Operant conditioning
b. Classical conditioning
c. Self-perception
d. None of these

**Answer: c) Self-perception**
Explanation: Bem's self-perception theory says attitudes are learned by observing our own behaviour.

**12.** Theory of cognitive dissonance is offered by:

a. Skinner
b. Bandura
c. Festinger
d. None of these

**Answer: c) Festinger**
Explanation: Festinger proposed cognitive dissonance.

**13.** The ability to view the world in 3 dimensions and perceive distance is:

a. Depth perception
b. Illusion
c. Delusion
d. None of these

**Answer: a) Depth perception**
Explanation: Depth perception is seeing the world in three dimensions.

**14.** According to Bandura, one is socialized by:

a. Punishment
b. Observation
c. Food
d. None of these

**Answer: b) Observation**
Explanation: Bandura says we are socialised by observation.

**15.** Crowding causes increase in heart rate and the level of adrenaline:

a. No
b. Yes

**16.** Studies demonstrate that greater genetic similarity is associated with greater similarity of attitudes:

a. True
b. False

**17.** A theory that states that emotions are a joint result of non-specific physiological arousal and interpretation of the arousal is called:

a. Two-factor theory of emotion
b. James-Lange theory of emotion
c. Bem's theory of emotion
d. None of these

**Answer: a) Two-factor theory of emotion**
Explanation: The two-factor theory (Schachter and Singer) combines arousal and its interpretation.

**18.** Maintenance of an internal biological balance is called:

a. Instinct
b. Need
c. Homeostasis
d. None of these

**Answer: c) Homeostasis**
Explanation: Homeostasis is the maintenance of internal balance.

**19.** The part of personality that provides a buffer between the id and the outside world is:

a. Super-ego
b. Ego
c. Ego-ideal
d. None of these

**Answer: b) Ego**
Explanation: The ego mediates between the id and the outside world.

**20.** Specialized cells of nervous system carry:

a. Messages
b. Reflexes
c. Both (a) and (b)
d. Neither (a) nor (b)

**Answer: a) Messages**
Explanation: Specialised nerve cells carry messages.

## Set 2

**1.** The combination of responses or ideas in novel way is called:

a. Exploration
b. Creativity
c. Thinking

**Answer: b) Creativity**
Explanation: Creativity is combining ideas in a novel way.

**2.** Group intelligence testing is better than individual testing:

a. True
b. False

**3.** Birth typically occurs after:

a. Forty weeks conception
b. Thirty eight weeks conception
c. Four weeks conception
d. None of these

**Answer: a) Forty weeks conception**
Explanation: Birth is typically at about forty weeks.

**4.** Longitudinal researches investigate:

a. Behavior through time as subjects age
b. Behavior of different ages are compared
c. None of these

**Answer: a) Behavior through time as subjects age**
Explanation: Longitudinal studies follow the same subjects over time as they age.

**5.** The cause of abnormal behavior is only physiological, it is explained by:

a. Psycho model
b. Medical model
c. Psychosocial model
d. None of these

**Answer: b) Medical model**
Explanation: The medical model explains abnormal behaviour as physiological.

**6.** One abnormal behavior was associated with witchcraft:

a. True
b. False

**7.** A feeling of apprehension or tension is:

a. Frustration
b. Panic
c. Anxiety

**Answer: c) Anxiety**
Explanation: Anxiety is a feeling of apprehension or tension.

**8.** Which of the following treatments deal with phobias by gradual exposure?

a. Superego control
b. Systematic desensitization
c. Reinforcement
d. Dreams
e. None of these

**Answer: b) Systematic desensitization**
Explanation: Systematic desensitisation treats phobias by gradual exposure.

**9.** The operation of the brain's neurotransmitters is altered because:

a. Use of LSD
b. Use of alcohol
c. None of these

**10.** Researches show that poor performance is the result of:

a. Poor conditions
b. Job satisfaction
c. Both (a) and (b)
d. None of these

**11.** Low job satisfaction is likely to bring about high absenteeism:

a. False
b. True

**12.** The term propinquity explains:

a. Geographical proximity
b. Why people affiliate with one another
c. Both (a) and (b)
d. None of these

**Answer: c) Both (a) and (b)**
Explanation: Propinquity is geographical nearness, which is why people affiliate.

**13.** Groupthink refers to:

a. Deterioration of mental efficiency
b. Deterioration of physical energy
c. Think tank
d. None of these

**Answer: a) Deterioration of mental efficiency**
Explanation: Groupthink is a deterioration of mental efficiency and judgement in cohesive groups.

**14.** Main functions of stimulants are:

a. To alleviate tension
b. To provide energy and alertness
c. Prescribed for insomnia
d. None of these

**Answer: b) To provide energy and alertness**
Explanation: Stimulants provide energy and alertness.

**15.** Chromosomal anomalies can be recognized by slanting eyes and flat nose:

a. Down's syndrome
b. PKU
c. Langdon Down's syndrome

**Answer: a) Down's syndrome**
Explanation: Slanting eyes and a flat nose point to Down's syndrome.

**16.** Character disorders, such as obsessive-compulsive behavior, are developed due to disturbed parent-child relations in the:

a. Later Oedipal phase
b. Paranoid stage
c. Libido
d. None of these

**17.** According to Piaget, from 2 to 7 years of age, language is developed slowly and gradually — this stage is called:

a. Preoperational stage
b. Egocentric stage
c. Sensorimotor stage
d. None of these

**Answer: a) Preoperational stage**
Explanation: From 2 to 7 years is Piaget's preoperational stage.

**18.** Speech-like but meaningless sound that appears between 3 months to one year is termed as:

a. Syntax
b. Babble
c. Conversation
d. None of these

**Answer: b) Babble**
Explanation: Babbling is speech-like meaningless sound in infancy.

**19.** The ability to deal with new problems and encounters is technically called as:

a. Crystallized intelligence
b. g-factor of intelligence
c. Fluid intelligence
d. None of these

**Answer: c) Fluid intelligence**
Explanation: Fluid intelligence is the ability to deal with new problems.

**20.** The triarchic theory of intelligence suggests that there are:

a. Four components
b. Six components
c. Three components
d. None of these

**Answer: c) Three components**
Explanation: Sternberg's triarchic theory has three components: analytical, creative and practical.
