**1.** Which of the following is likely to be associated with antisocial behavior?

a. Being an only child
b. Being born to a teenage mother
c. Prenatal maternal smoking
d. Maternal mental health problems
e. Low maternal IQ

**Answer: b) Being born to a teenage mother**
Explanation: Being born to a teenage mother is a recognised risk factor for antisocial behaviour.

**2.** Which of the following is **not** a predictor of delinquency:

a. Family criminality
b. Family poverty
c. Large family size
d. Harsh parenting style
e. Authoritative parenting style

**Answer: e) Authoritative parenting style**
Explanation: Authoritative parenting protects against delinquency; it is not a predictor of it.

**3.** The following are true of effective parental rearing practices, **except**:

a. Reward and punishment should be immediate
b. Praise should outweigh punishment
c. Discipline demands should be reasonable and consistent
d. Parents are usually under tension and disharmony
e. Authoritative discipline is important

**Answer: d) Parents are usually under tension and disharmony**
Explanation: Tension and disharmony in parents are not part of effective rearing.

**4.** Which of the following is **not** a characteristic of role problems?

a. Parent–child role reversal
b. Overprotection — child becomes emotionally disabled
c. Role reversal
d. Unequal distribution of roles
e. Psychological disturbance

**Answer: e) Psychological disturbance**
Explanation: Psychological disturbance is a consequence of role problems, not a characteristic of them.

**5.** The following areas of the brain have a blood–brain barrier:

a. Pineal gland
b. Cortex
c. Pituitary gland
d. The endothelium of the choroid plexuses
e. The synapses

**Answer: b) Cortex**
Explanation: The cortex has a blood-brain barrier; the pineal and pituitary glands do not.

**6.** The ability of a molecule to pass from the blood into the brain is based on:

a. Its concentration gradient between brain and blood
b. Its permeability
c. Lipid solubility
d. The presence of an active transport mechanism for the molecule
e. All of the above

**Answer: e) All of the above**
Explanation: Concentration gradient, permeability, lipid solubility and active transport all matter.

**7.** Emotional reactions are recognized as the primary function of the:

a. Basal ganglia
b. Thalamus
c. Internal capsule
d. Limbic system
e. Reticular activating system

**Answer: d) Limbic system**
Explanation: The limbic system is the centre of emotional reactions.

**8.** The most basic unit of the human cell that carries information on body characteristics is called:

a. Gamete
b. Chromosome
c. DNA
d. RNA
e. Gene

**Answer: e) Gene**
Explanation: The gene is the basic unit of inheritance of body characteristics.

**38.** An external stimulus such as hunger, thirst and sex — common to every animal — is called:

**39.** According to the James–Lange Theory, emotional behavior:

**53.** Your knowledge of the alphabet and multiplication tables is stored in your ______ memory.
