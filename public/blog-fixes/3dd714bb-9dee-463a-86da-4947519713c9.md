## School of Medicine — MBChB Year III (Sep 2023 Intake)### End of Year Examinations | 14th – 24th July 2026---
The Mount Kenya University School of Medicine End of Year Examinations for the Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBChB) Year III, September 2023 Intake, are scheduled to run from 14th to 24th July 2026. The examination period covers a wide range of core medical sciences including Pathology, Microbiology, Pharmacology, Clinical Medicine, and Community Health. Students are expected to sit written papers, practical examinations, and viva voce assessments across two weeks. The viva examinations will be held at the GKFH venue. Below is the complete timetable followed by a detailed breakdown of topics to cover for each unit.
