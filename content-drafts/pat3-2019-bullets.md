# PAT III — Progress Assessment Test (8 November 2019)

*Spot-style revision set, University of Nairobi, Dept. of Human Anatomy. 50 questions, upper limb, histology and embryology, with answers.*

## Q1: Name the tagged nerve and state its root value
![Q1 - dissection of the arm with a nerve tagged](__IMG:pat3-2019/q01.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Medial brachial cutaneous nerve
- Root value: C8, T1

## Q2: State two muscles innervated by the nerve tagged
![Q2 - axillary dissection, nerve tagged](__IMG:pat3-2019/q02.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Coracobrachialis
- Brachialis (medial part)
- Biceps brachii

## Q3: Name the vessel tagged and state its extents
![Q3 - forceps tagging a vessel in the axilla](__IMG:pat3-2019/q03.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Axillary artery
- Extent: from the lateral border of the first rib to the inferior border of teres major

## Q4: State the root value of the nerve tagged and two muscles it innervates
![Q4 - probe tagging a nerve at the shoulder](__IMG:pat3-2019/q04.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Axillary nerve
- Root value: C5, C6
- Supplies deltoid and teres minor

## Q5: State the root value of the nerve tagged and its distribution
![Q5 - probe tagging a nerve in the axilla](__IMG:pat3-2019/q05.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Intercostobrachial nerve (lateral cutaneous branch of the 2nd intercostal nerve)
- Root value: T2
- Distribution: skin of the floor of the axilla and upper medial arm

## Q6: State innervation and two actions of the muscle that inserts in the part shown
![Q6 - humerus with an attachment site pinned](__IMG:pat3-2019/q06.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Latissimus dorsi (inserts on the intertubercular/bicipital groove)
- Innervation: thoracodorsal nerve
- Actions: medial rotation, extension, adduction of the shoulder

## Q7: Name the muscle X and state its innervation
![Q7 - back dissection, muscle marked X](__IMG:pat3-2019/q07.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Longissimus (part of erector spinae)
- Innervation: dorsal rami of spinal nerves

## Q8: Name two contents of the space probed
![Q8 - probe in the deltopectoral triangle](__IMG:pat3-2019/q08.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Deltopectoral triangle
- Deltoid branch of the thoracoacromial artery
- Cephalic vein
- Deltopectoral (infraclavicular) lymph nodes

## Q9: Name a muscle that (a) originates and (b) inserts on the part probed, and state their innervation
![Q9 - scapula specimen with a probed point](__IMG:pat3-2019/q09.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- (a) Coracobrachialis and short head of biceps brachii originate from the coracoid process — musculocutaneous nerve
- (b) Pectoralis minor inserts on the coracoid process — medial pectoral nerve

## Q10: State the origin and innervation of the muscle probed
![Q10 - probe on a chest wall muscle](__IMG:pat3-2019/q10.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Subclavius
- Origin: 1st costochondral junction
- Innervation: nerve to subclavius

## Q11: Identify A and B
![Q11 - shoulder dissection with A and B labelled](__IMG:pat3-2019/q11.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- A: long head of biceps brachii tendon
- B: coracohumeral ligament

## Q12: State 2 actions of E and origin of F
![Q12 - arm dissection, E and F labelled](__IMG:pat3-2019/q12.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- E (biceps brachii): flexes the elbow and shoulder
- E (biceps brachii): supinates the forearm when the elbow is midflexed
- F (medial cord of the brachial plexus): roots C7, C8, T1

## Q13: State 4 sites of entrapment of A
![Q13 - probe tagging a nerve at the arm, labelled A](__IMG:pat3-2019/q13.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- A: radial nerve
- Arcade of Frohse
- Spiral (radial) groove of the humerus
- Lateral intermuscular septum
- Between brachialis and brachioradialis

## Q14: State the termination of the artery labelled V
![Q14 - artery labelled V in a forearm dissection](__IMG:pat3-2019/q14.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Brachial artery
- Terminates by bifurcating into the ulnar and radial arteries
- At the level of the neck of the radius, in the cubital fossa

## Q15: State the neurovascular structure most at risk due to a fracture at the tagged part and give the basis
![Q15 - humerus with a fracture site pinned](__IMG:pat3-2019/q15.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Brachial artery
- Basis: tethered as it passes through the fibrous arch of pronator teres / distal humerus region — can be compressed or lacerated by displaced fracture fragments

## Q16: Identify structures A and B
![Q16 - shoulder MRI with A and B labelled](__IMG:pat3-2019/q16.jpg__)

## Q17: State the structure tagged and its clinical utility

## Q41: State the anomaly depicted and give its basis
![Q41 - grape-like translucent vesicles](__IMG:pat3-2019/q41.jpg__)

**Answer:**

## Q42: State the anomaly depicted and give its basis
![Q42 - conjoined infants](__IMG:pat3-2019/q42.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Conjoined twins (here: omphalopagus)
- Basis: incomplete separation of the embryonic disc at the primitive streak stage

## Q48: State 2 functions of B

## Q49: Identify the anomaly and state its basis
![Q49 - compressed, mummified fetus](__IMG:pat3-2019/q49.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Fetus papyraceus
- Basis: death and compression/mummification of one fetus in a multiple pregnancy

## Q50: Identify the anomaly and state its basis
![Q50 - H-shaped diagram of the gut tube and a fistulous tract](__IMG:pat3-2019/q50.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Vitelline fistula
- Basis: persistence of the vitelline (omphalomesenteric) duct, which normally obliterates by week 7
