# PAT III — Progress Assessment Test (8 November 2019)

*Spot-style revision set, University of Nairobi, Dept. of Human Anatomy. 50 questions, upper limb, histology and embryology, with answers.*

## Q1: Name the tagged nerve and state its root value
![Q1 - dissection of the arm with a nerve tagged](__IMG:pat3-2019/q01.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Medial brachial cutaneous nerve
- Root value: C8
- Root value: T1
- Arises from the medial cord of the brachial plexus
- Supplies skin over the medial side of the arm

## Q2: State two muscles innervated by the nerve tagged
![Q2 - axillary dissection, nerve tagged](__IMG:pat3-2019/q02.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Coracobrachialis
- Brachialis (medial part)
- Biceps brachii

## Q3: Name the vessel tagged and state its extents
![Q3 - forceps tagging a vessel in the axilla](__IMG:pat3-2019/q03.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Axillary artery
- Begins: lateral border of the first rib
- Ends: inferior border of teres major
- Continues as the brachial artery beyond teres major
- Divided into 3 parts by pectoralis minor

## Q4: State the root value of the nerve tagged and two muscles it innervates
![Q4 - probe tagging a nerve at the shoulder](__IMG:pat3-2019/q04.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Axillary nerve
- Root value: C5
- Root value: C6
- Supplies deltoid
- Supplies teres minor
- Also supplies skin over the "regimental badge" area of the shoulder

## Q5: State the root value of the nerve tagged and its distribution
![Q5 - probe tagging a nerve in the axilla](__IMG:pat3-2019/q05.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Intercostobrachial nerve
- Lateral cutaneous branch of the 2nd intercostal nerve
- Root value: T2
- Distribution: skin of the floor of the axilla
- Distribution: skin of the upper medial arm
- Clinically relevant in axillary lymph node clearance (risk of injury)

## Q6: State innervation and two actions of the muscle that inserts in the part shown
![Q6 - humerus with an attachment site pinned](__IMG:pat3-2019/q06.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Latissimus dorsi
- Inserts on the intertubercular (bicipital) groove of the humerus
- Innervation: thoracodorsal nerve
- Action: medially rotates the shoulder
- Action: extends the shoulder
- Action: adducts the shoulder

## Q7: Name the muscle X and state its innervation
![Q7 - back dissection, muscle marked X](__IMG:pat3-2019/q07.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Longissimus
- Part of erector spinae
- Innervation: dorsal rami of spinal nerves
- Other erector spinae components: iliocostalis (lateral), spinalis (medial)

## Q8: Name two contents of the space probed
![Q8 - probe in the deltopectoral triangle](__IMG:pat3-2019/q08.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Deltopectoral triangle
- Deltoid branch of the thoracoacromial artery
- Cephalic vein
- Deltopectoral (infraclavicular) lymph nodes
- Boundaries: deltoid, pectoralis major, clavicle

## Q9: Name a muscle that (a) originates and (b) inserts on the part probed, and state their innervation
![Q9 - scapula specimen with a probed point](__IMG:pat3-2019/q09.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- (a) Coracobrachialis
- Originates from the coracoid process
- (a) Short head of biceps brachii also originates here
- Innervation of both: musculocutaneous nerve
- (b) Pectoralis minor inserts on the coracoid process
- Innervation: medial pectoral nerve

## Q10: State the origin and innervation of the muscle probed

## Q11: Identify A and B

## Q12: State 2 actions of E and origin of F

## Q13: State 4 sites of entrapment of A
![Q13 - probe tagging a nerve at the arm, labelled A](__IMG:pat3-2019/q13.jpg__)

## Q14: State the termination of the artery labelled V
![Q14 - artery labelled V in a forearm dissection](__IMG:pat3-2019/q14.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Brachial artery
- Terminates by bifurcating into the ulnar artery
- Terminates by bifurcating into the radial artery
- Level: neck of the radius, in the cubital fossa

## Q15: State the neurovascular structure most at risk due to a fracture at the tagged part and give the basis
![Q15 - humerus with a fracture site pinned](__IMG:pat3-2019/q15.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Brachial artery
- Basis: tethered as it passes through the fibrous arch of pronator teres / distal humerus region
- Can be compressed or lacerated by displaced fracture fragments
- Also at risk nearby: median nerve

## Q16: Identify structures A and B

## Q41: State the anomaly depicted and give its basis
![Q41 - grape-like translucent vesicles](__IMG:pat3-2019/q41.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Molar pregnancy / hydatidiform mole
- Basis: the paternal set of chromosomes outnumbers/predominates over the maternal set
- Complete mole: entirely paternal (androgenetic), diploid
- Partial mole: triploid, from dispermy

## Q42: State the anomaly depicted and give its basis
![Q42 - conjoined infants](__IMG:pat3-2019/q42.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Conjoined twins (here: omphalopagus)
- Basis: incomplete separation of the embryonic disc at the primitive streak stage
- Occurs with monozygotic twinning splitting after day 13

## Q43: Identify anomaly X and state an embryonic structure related to its formation
![Q43 - sagittal MRI of the lower spine showing a mass](__IMG:pat3-2019/q43.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- X: chordoma
- Related structure: the notochord
- Chordomas arise from notochordal remnants
- Typically found along the midline, from skull base to sacrum

## Q48: State 2 functions of B
![Q48 - trilaminar embryonic disc diagram, primitive node, A and B labelled](__IMG:pat3-2019/q48.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- B: endoderm
- Forms the primitive (embryonic) gut
- Gives rise to primordial gut-derived epithelial cells/organs
- Also gives rise to: lining of the respiratory tract, liver, pancreas, bladder

## Q49: Identify the anomaly and state its basis
![Q49 - compressed, mummified fetus](__IMG:pat3-2019/q49.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Fetus papyraceus
- Basis: death and compression/mummification of one fetus in a multiple pregnancy
- The surviving twin continues to develop normally

## Q50: Identify the anomaly and state its basis
![Q50 - H-shaped diagram of the gut tube and a fistulous tract](__IMG:pat3-2019/q50.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Vitelline fistula
- Basis: persistence of the vitelline (omphalomesenteric) duct
- Normally obliterates by week 7
- Related anomaly (partial persistence): Meckel's diverticulum
