# PAT III — Progress Assessment Test (8 November 2019)

*Spot-style revision set, University of Nairobi, Dept. of Human Anatomy. 50 questions, upper limb, histology and embryology, with answers.*

## Q1: Name the tagged nerve and state its root value
![Q1 - dissection of the arm with a nerve tagged](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q01.jpg)

**Answer:** Medial brachial cutaneous nerve; root value C8, T1.

## Q2: State two muscles innervated by the nerve tagged
![Q2 - axillary dissection, nerve tagged](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q02.jpg)

**Answer:** Coracobrachialis, brachialis (medial part) and biceps brachii.

## Q3: Name the vessel tagged and state its extents
![Q3 - forceps tagging a vessel in the axilla](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q03.jpg)

**Answer:** Axillary artery; extends from the lateral border of the first rib to the inferior border of teres major.

## Q4: State the root value of the nerve tagged and two muscles it innervates
![Q4 - probe tagging a nerve at the shoulder](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q04.jpg)

**Answer:** Axillary nerve; root value C5, C6. Supplies deltoid and teres minor.

## Q5: State the root value of the nerve tagged and its distribution
![Q5 - probe tagging a nerve in the axilla](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q05.jpg)

**Answer:** Intercostobrachial nerve (lateral cutaneous branch of the 2nd intercostal nerve); root value T2. Distribution: skin of the floor of the axilla and upper medial arm.

## Q6: State innervation and two actions of the muscle that inserts in the part shown
![Q6 - humerus with an attachment site pinned](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q06.jpg)

**Answer:** Latissimus dorsi, inserting on the intertubercular (bicipital) groove of the humerus. Innervation: thoracodorsal nerve. Actions: medial rotation, extension and adduction of the shoulder.

## Q7: Name the muscle X and state its innervation
![Q7 - back dissection, muscle marked X](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q07.jpg)

**Answer:** Longissimus (part of erector spinae). Innervation: dorsal rami of spinal nerves.

## Q8: Name two contents of the space probed
![Q8 - probe in the deltopectoral triangle](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q08.jpg)

**Answer:** Deltopectoral triangle — contains the deltoid branch of the thoracoacromial artery, the cephalic vein, and the deltopectoral (infraclavicular) lymph nodes.

## Q9: Name a muscle that (a) originates and (b) inserts on the part probed, and state their innervation
![Q9 - scapula specimen with a probed point](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q09.jpg)

**Answer:** (a) Coracobrachialis and the short head of biceps brachii originate from the coracoid process — musculocutaneous nerve. (b) Pectoralis minor inserts on the coracoid process — medial pectoral nerve.

## Q10: State the origin and innervation of the muscle probed
![Q10 - probe on a chest wall muscle](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q10.jpg)

**Answer:** Subclavius. Origin: 1st costochondral junction. Innervation: nerve to subclavius.

## Q11: Identify A and B
![Q11 - shoulder dissection with A and B labelled](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q11.jpg)

**Answer:** A: long head of biceps brachii tendon. B: coracohumeral ligament.

## Q12: State 2 actions of E and origin of F
![Q12 - arm dissection, E and F labelled](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q12.jpg)

**Answer:** E (biceps brachii): flexion of the elbow and shoulder; supinates the forearm when the elbow is midflexed. F (medial cord of the brachial plexus): roots C7, C8, T1.

## Q13: State 4 sites of entrapment of A
![Q13 - probe tagging a nerve at the arm, labelled A](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q13.jpg)

**Answer:** Radial nerve — arcade of Frohse, the spiral (radial) groove of the humerus, the lateral intermuscular septum, and between brachialis and brachioradialis.

## Q14: State the termination of the artery labelled V
![Q14 - artery labelled V in a forearm dissection](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q14.jpg)

**Answer:** Ulnar and radial arteries (brachial artery terminates by bifurcating into these two at the level of the neck of the radius, in the cubital fossa).

## Q15: State the neurovascular structure most at risk due to a fracture at the tagged part and give the basis
![Q15 - humerus with a fracture site pinned](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q15.jpg)

**Answer:** Brachial artery. Basis: it is tethered as it passes through the fibrous arch of pronator teres / distal humerus region and can be compressed or lacerated by displaced fracture fragments.

## Q16: Identify structures A and B
![Q16 - shoulder MRI with A and B labelled](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q16.jpg)

**Answer:** A: supraspinatus (tendon). B: acromion.

## Q17: State the structure tagged and its clinical utility
![Q17 - pin at the cubital fossa](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q17.jpg)

**Answer:** Median cubital vein; clinical utility: intravenous access/venepuncture.

## Q18: State 2 ligaments associated with the joint probed
![Q18 - probe at the elbow joint](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q18.jpg)

**Answer:** Quadrate ligament, annular ligament (and the radial collateral ligament).

## Q19: Identify structures 3, 4, 5 and 7
![Q19 - wrist X-ray with carpal bones numbered](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q19.jpg)

**Answer:** 3: pisiform. 4: trapezium. 5: trapezoid. 7: hamate.

## Q20: Name the structures A, B, C and D
![Q20 - forearm dissection with A, B, C, D labelled](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q20.jpg)

**Answer:** A: pronator quadratus. B: median nerve. C: anterior interosseous nerve. D: anterior interosseous nerve (accompanying vessels/second branch shown).

## Q21: Name the terminal branches of the structure below
![Q21 - palmar dissection with a probe](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q21.jpg)

**Answer:** Radial artery — princeps pollicis artery, radialis indicis artery, and the deep palmar arch.

## Q22: Identify the muscle tagged and its action
![Q22 - probe on a forearm muscle](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q22.jpg)

**Answer:** Brachioradialis; flexes the elbow joint, and supinates the forearm from a pronated position (to neutral/mid-flexed position).

## Q23: State the communications of the space labelled X
![Q23 - diagram of the palm with a fascial space labelled X](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q23.jpg)

**Answer:** Web space (interdigital) proximally continuous with the space of Parona distally, at the wrist/forearm.

## Q24: Identify the nerve below and its nerve roots
![Q24 - forearm dissection, central nerve](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q24.jpg)

**Answer:** Radial nerve; roots C5–C8, T1.

## Q25: State two muscles that form the floor of the space below
![Q25 - labelled cubital fossa prosection](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q25.jpg)

**Answer:** Brachialis and supinator.

## Q26: Name the branches of the structure labelled X
![Q26 - probe tagging a vessel in the proximal forearm](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q26.jpg)

**Answer:** Ulnar artery — common interosseous artery (dividing into anterior and posterior interosseous arteries), and the ulnar recurrent artery.

## Q27: Identify the tagged bone and a muscular attachment to it
![Q27 - wrist skeleton with a carpal bone tagged X](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q27.jpg)

**Answer:** Pisiform; the tendon of flexor carpi ulnaris attaches to it.

## Q28: State two actions of the tagged muscle
![Q28 - probe in the palm](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q28.jpg)

**Answer:** Lumbricals — extend the interphalangeal joints and flex the metacarpophalangeal joints.

## Q29: Identify the muscle below and its innervation
![Q29 - thenar eminence dissection](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q29.jpg)

**Answer:** Abductor pollicis brevis; innervated by the recurrent branch of the median nerve.

## Q30: State (a) the site of entrapment of the nerve below at the wrist, (b) its nerve roots
![Q30 - nerve at the wrist crease](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q30.jpg)

**Answer:** Ulnar nerve. (a) Guyon's canal. (b) Roots C7, C8, T1.

## Q31: State two characteristic features of the tissue displayed
![Q31 - skeletal muscle histology](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q31.jpg)

**Answer:** Skeletal muscle — cross-striations, multinucleated fibres, elongated cells with peripheral nuclei.

## Q32: Giving reason, identify the epithelial type displayed
![Q32 - ciliated epithelium over connective tissue](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q32.jpg)

**Answer:** Pseudostratified columnar ciliated epithelium. Reason: cilia are visible on the surface, and there is a lack of nuclei in the apical one-third of the epithelium (nuclei sit at varying levels, all cells reach the basement membrane).

## Q33: Identify A and B
![Q33 - bone histology with A and B labelled](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q33.jpg)

**Answer:** A: Haversian canal. B: osteocytes in lacunae.

## Q34: Identify zones labelled A and D
![Q34 - growth plate histology with zones a and d](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q34.jpg)

**Answer:** A: resting zone. D: zone of calcification.

## Q35: State two sites where the tissue displayed is found
![Q35 - mucoid connective tissue](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q35.jpg)

**Answer:** Wharton's jelly of the umbilical cord, dental pulp of a developing tooth, and the nucleus pulposus of the intervertebral disc.

## Q36: Name 1, 2, 3 and 4
![Q36 - electron micrograph of a sarcomere](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q36.jpg)

**Answer:** 1: Z-disc. 2: A-band. 3: M-line. 4: I-band.

## Q37: Give the salient features of the tissue displayed
![Q37 - cartilage histology](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q37.jpg)

**Answer:** Elastic cartilage — abundant elastic fibres in the matrix, and large lacunae housing chondrocytes.

## Q38: Identify epithelium displayed and where it is found
![Q38 - transitional epithelium histology](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q38.jpg)

**Answer:** Transitional (urinary) epithelium; found lining the renal calyces, ureters, and urinary bladder.

## Q39: State major collagen types in the tissue displayed
![Q39 - connective tissue histology](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q39.jpg)

**Answer:** Type I and type II collagen.

## Q40: Name cell types found in region a
![Q40 - bone marrow/ossification histology with region a marked](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q40.jpg)

**Answer:** Osteoclasts, osteocytes, osteoblasts, and osteoprogenitor cells.

## Q41: State the anomaly depicted and give its basis
![Q41 - grape-like translucent vesicles](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q41.jpg)

**Answer:** Molar pregnancy / hydatidiform mole. Basis: the paternal set of chromosomes outnumbers/predominates over the maternal set.

## Q42: State the anomaly depicted and give its basis
![Q42 - conjoined infants](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q42.jpg)

**Answer:** Conjoined twins (Siamese twins) — here, omphalopagus. Basis: incomplete separation of the embryonic disc at the primitive streak stage.

## Q43: Identify anomaly X and state an embryonic structure related to its formation
![Q43 - sagittal MRI of the lower spine showing a mass](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q43.jpg)

**Answer:** X: chordoma. Related structure: the notochord (chordomas arise from notochordal remnants).

## Q44: State the anomalies labelled A and B
![Q44 - diagram comparing two spina bifida variants](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q44.jpg)

**Answer:** A: spina bifida — meningocele. B: spina bifida — meningomyelocele.

## Q45: Identify A and B
![Q45 - two placental specimens](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q45.jpg)

**Answer:** A: battledore placenta (marginal cord insertion). B: velamentous insertion of the umbilical cord.

## Q46: State the procedures labelled A and B
![Q46 - diagrams of two prenatal sampling procedures](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q46.jpg)

**Answer:** A: amniocentesis. B: chorionic villus sampling.

## Q47: Identify placental attachment depicted in A & B
![Q47 - diagram of abnormal placental adherence](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q47.jpg)

**Answer:** A: placenta increta. B: placenta percreta.

## Q48: State 2 functions of B
![Q48 - trilaminar embryonic disc diagram, primitive node, A and B labelled](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q48.jpg)

**Answer:** B: endoderm — forms the primitive (embryonic) gut, and gives rise to primordial gut-derived epithelial cells/organs.

## Q49: Identify the anomaly and state its basis
![Q49 - compressed, mummified fetus](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q49.jpg)

**Answer:** Fetus papyraceus. Basis: death and compression/mummification of one fetus in a multiple pregnancy.

## Q50: Identify the anomaly and state its basis
![Q50 - H-shaped diagram of the gut tube and a fistulous tract](/images/year1/anatomy/aponeurosis-01/pat3-2019/q50.jpg)

**Answer:** Vitelline fistula. Basis: persistence of the vitelline (omphalomesenteric) duct, which normally obliterates by week 7.
