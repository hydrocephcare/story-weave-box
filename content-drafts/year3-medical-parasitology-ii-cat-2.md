## Verified correction notes

| Question | Verified finding | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | **C. *Spirometra theileri*** | Sparganosis is caused by plerocercoid larvae of *Spirometra* species, not *Taenia saginata*. |
| 8 | **No valid option** | Clonazepam may be used for seizure control in neurocysticercosis caused by larval *Taenia solium*; pork tapeworm is absent from the choices. |
| 9 | **C. *Toxocara canis*** | Ocular toxocariasis can produce unilateral visual disease and strabismus. |
| 13 | **No valid option** | Swimmer's itch is caused predominantly by non-human, especially avian, schistosome cercariae. *Schistosoma mekongi* is not the expected answer. |
| 15 | **Ambiguous** | Pruritus occurs with both pediculosis and phthiriasis; the stem lacks the site or exposure needed to distinguish *Pediculus* from *Pthirus*. |
| 21 | **Insufficiently specified** | Different *Aedes* surveillance traps use different visual cues. The trap type is required before one colour pair can be keyed. |
| 24 | **Association is not proof of transmission** | Bed bugs (*Cimex*) have been investigated for hepatitis B carriage, but wording that implies established biological transmission is misleading. |
| 26 | **A and C are both stages** | Hemimetabolous development is egg → nymph → adult. To ask for the stage replacing larva/pupa, the answer is **C. Nymph**. |
| 27 | **No valid option** | Tabanids are associated with agents such as *Loa loa*, trypanosomes, anthrax and tularaemia; none of the listed diseases is the expected association. |
| 29 | **C. *Aedes albopictus*** | *Aedes albopictus* commonly breeds in natural and artificial water-holding containers. |
| 30 | **A. *Anopheles stephensi*** | *Anopheles stephensi* was first detected in Kenya in 2022. |

Questions not listed above retain the source marking only as a provisional revision aid; they have not been promoted to a verified grading key.

## Fill-in revision answers

| Clinical clue | Parasite | Diagnostic evidence | Core treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| River blindness | *Onchocerca volvulus* | Microfilariae in a skin snip; ocular examination where indicated | Ivermectin; doxycycline may be used against *Wolbachia* in individual care |
| Delhi boil (Old World cutaneous leishmaniasis) | Usually *Leishmania tropica* | Amastigotes in lesion smear/biopsy or molecular confirmation | Local or systemic antileishmanial therapy according to lesion and setting |
| Pyosalpingitis / female genital schistosomiasis | *Schistosoma haematobium* is the relevant parasitic differential | Eggs in genital-tissue biopsy or supportive urine/serologic testing, interpreted clinically | Praziquantel |
| Neurocysticercosis | Larval *Taenia solium* | Neuroimaging plus clinical/serologic assessment | Control seizures and raised intracranial pressure first; selected viable cysts receive albendazole with corticosteroids, sometimes with praziquantel |
| Quartan malaria | *Plasmodium malariae* | Parasites on thick and thin blood films or a validated rapid/molecular test | Treat according to current national malaria guidance and local susceptibility |
