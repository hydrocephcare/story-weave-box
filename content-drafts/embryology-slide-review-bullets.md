## Number 1: Spermatogenesis — identify A–G

- B: primary spermatocyte

## Number 6: State the anomaly and its basis
![Number 6 - newborn with a sacrococcygeal mass](__IMG:embryology-slide-review/n06.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Sacrococcygeal teratoma
- Basis: failure of degeneration/regression of the primitive streak
- Typically contains tissue from all three germ layers (source key leaves the follow-up unanswered)
