## Q33: Identify the hormones plotted X, Y, Z, W (menstrual cycle correlation)

![Graph of four hormone curves across a 28-day cycle, labelled X, Y, Z, W, beside a reference diagram of the ovarian and uterine cycles](__IMG:anatomy-spot-revision/q33.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- X (sharp, tallest peak exactly at day 14): luteinizing hormone (LH) — the LH surge that triggers ovulation
- W (smaller peak coinciding with X, at day 14): follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH)
- Z (moderate peak just before day 14): oestradiol — oestrogen peaks shortly before the LH surge
- Y (broad peak around day 21, mid-luteal phase): progesterone — secreted by the corpus luteum

## Q37: State two fates of B; four results of longitudinal folding

![Diagram of the trilaminar embryo showing amniotic cavity (A), yolk sac (B), and connecting stalk (C), with the chorion labelled](__IMG:anatomy-spot-revision/q37.jpg__)

**Answer:**
- Two fates of B (yolk sac):

- Ventral part — degenerates

- Four results of longitudinal folding of the embryo:
- Primary curvature formation
- Descent of the heart
- Connecting stalk formation
- Amniotic cavity expansion
- Also accepted: oesophagus elongation
