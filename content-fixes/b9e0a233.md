# Behavioural Sciences SAQs — May 2013 (Past Paper)

*University of Nairobi, University Examinations 2012/2013. First Year Examinations for the Degree of Bachelor of Dental Surgery. VDS 130: Behavioural Science in Dentistry. 7 May 2013, 2.00 to 5.00 p.m. Questions only, typed from the scanned paper.*

---

## Section A (60 marks): answer ALL questions

**Write short notes on:**

**1. Steps in developmental psychology.** (10 marks)

**2. Factors that influence societal shifts in culture.** (10 marks)

**3. Psycho-social and cultural factors that influence pain experience and pain behaviour.** (10 marks)

**4. Short notes on:**

- (a) Agencies of change in secondary socialisation (5 marks)
- (b) Functions of a group to its members (5 marks)

**5. Short notes on:**

- (a) Factors that influence illness behaviour (5 marks)
- (b) Activities associated with health behaviour (5 marks)

**6. Giving examples, explain how nature and nurture may influence development of intelligence in human beings.** (10 marks)

## Section B (40 marks): answer ONLY TWO questions

**1. Health belief model and communication of innovations**

- (a) Discuss: (i) the health belief model (5 marks); (ii) the communication of innovations model (5 marks)
- (b) Comment on the application of the health belief model on anti-smoking campaigns for the prevention of oral cancer. (10 marks)

**2. The way a patient behaves during a dental visit has an effect on treatment outcomes.**

- (a) (i) What happens when there is an unresolved conflict in the patient for which the ego is not able to find a solution? (5 marks)
- (a) (ii) Draw the Lewis' models of the world as perceived by a patient at home and at the dental clinic. (8 marks)
- (b) Briefly describe the psychology of personality as stipulated by Freud. (7 marks)

**3. Maslow's hierarchy of needs**

- (a) Enumerate and explain the various hierarchy of human needs according to Abraham Maslow's model. (15 marks)
- (b) Comment on how you think this model would be useful to you in helping people improve in their work performance. (5 marks)
