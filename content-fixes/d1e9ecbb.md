(c) With illustration, explain the relationship between absorbance and concentration of a given absorbing medium?
The higher the concentration of a given solution the higher the absorbance this is due to higher number of particles per unit volume hence more light is absorbed by the particles.
e.g. 1 mole of hydrochloric acid in 500ml of water
& 1 mole of hydrochloric acid in 1000ml of water
The solution A obtained has higher absorbance as compared to the solution B, this is because the number of hydrochloric particles per unit volume of water in solution A is higher than in solution B thus greater Absorbance of solution A, when a ray of monochromatic light is passed through it.
