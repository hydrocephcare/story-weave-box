# Behavioural Sciences SAQs — December 2013 (Past Paper)

*University of Nairobi, University Examinations 2013/2014. Level I Examinations for the Degree of Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery. Behavioural Sciences, Paper II: Short Answer Questions (SAQ). 10 December 2013, 2.00 to 5.00 p.m. Questions only, typed from the scanned paper.*

---

**Instructions:** answer all questions. Answer all questions in a single booklet; use an additional booklet only when necessary.

**1. The family**

- (a) Define the family. (5 marks)
- (b) List 4 different forms of family. (5 marks)
- (c) Briefly describe two forms of the family listed above. (10 marks)
- (d) Give 5 reasons why you think the family is an important institution in society. (5 marks)

**2. Social conflict**

- (a) Define social conflict. (6 marks)
- (b) List five types of common social conflicts. (6 marks)
- (c) List 4 ways in which conflict can be resolved. (5 marks)
- (d) Describe the social conflict theory. (8 marks)

**3. Psychology and personality**

- (a) What is psychology? (5 marks)
- (b) List 4 theories of personality. (5 marks)
- (c) Explain one of the theories. (5 marks)
- (d) Differentiate in vivo/systematic desensitisation from flooding as practised in the behaviour therapy models, with examples. (10 marks)

**4. Delinquency**

- (a) Define delinquency. (5 marks)
- (b) Give 2 examples of delinquent behaviours. (5 marks)
- (c) List 5 environmental factors associated with delinquency. (5 marks)
- (d) List individual risk factors in delinquency. (5 marks)
- (e) What are the individual protective factors in delinquency? (5 marks)

**5. Child abuse**

- (a) Define child abuse. (5 marks)
- (b) List 4 forms of child abuse. (5 marks)
- (c) State 4 risk factors in child abuse. (5 marks)
- (d) List 4 factors in prevention of child abuse. (5 marks)
- (e) List 4 factors that may contribute to the success in prevention of child abuse. (5 marks)

**6. Define the following concepts**

- (a) Conditioned stimulus (5 marks)
- (b) Unconditioned stimulus (5 marks)
- (c) Conditioned response (5 marks)
- (d) Unconditioned response (5 marks)
- (e) Memory (5 marks)

**7. Learning and memory**

- (a) List four differences between classical vs operant conditioning. (10 marks)
- (b) Name and briefly describe 4 memory models. (15 marks)
