**1. A positive redox potential means a compound has;**

A. Lower affinity for electron
B. Higher affinity for electron
C. Lower affinity for proton
D. Higher affinity for proton
E. No affinity for proton or electron

**Answer: B.** Higher affinity for electron

**2. Oxidation of a molecule involves;**

A. Gain of electron
B. Loss of electron
C. Gain of proton
D. Loss of proton
E. None of the above

**Answer: B.** Loss of electron

**3. How many molecules of ATP are synthesized per NADH oxidized?**

A. 2
B. 3
C. 1
D. 4
E. 5

**Answer: B.** 3

**4. How many ATPS are produced by oxidation of 1 molecule of acetyl CoA?**

A. 8
B. 10
C. 12
D. 14
E. 16

**5. A kinase is an enzyme that;**

A. Uses NADH to change the oxidation state of a substrate
B. Uses ATP to fix a carbon to a substrate
C. Removes water from a double bond
D. Removes phosphate groups of substrates
E. Uses ATP to add a phosphate group to a substrate

**Answer: E.** Uses ATP to add a phosphate group to a substrate

**6. In the glycogen synthase reaction, the precursor to glycogen is;**

A. G6P
B. G1P
C. UDP-glucose
D. UTP-glucose
E. CDP-glucose

**Answer: C.** UDP-glucose

**7. Pyruvate is initially converted to which of the following in gluconeogenesis?**

A. Glycerol
B. PEP

D. AcetylCoA
E. Lactate

**Answer: C.** OAA

**8. The damage caused by UV light on DNA is:**

A. Depuration
B. Thymine dimerization
C. Loss of pyrimidine bases

**9. On a gene sequence, the correct amino acid start code is:**
