## Section A: Multiple Choice Questions

**1. The optimal condition that exists within the body is called:**

A. The extra-cellular space
B. The intra-cellular space
C. Homeostasis
D. The internal environment
E. None of the above

**Answer: C.** Homeostasis

**2. In the theoretical physiological control model, the controlled variable:**

A. Remains completely constant
B. Is a measurable entity
C. Is always measured
D. Is the input to the controlled system
E. Is the same as the forcing functions

**Answer: B.** Is a measurable entity

**3. In a positive feedback control system, the variable:**

A. Rises continuously
B. Falls then rises
C. Rises until the system breaks down
D. Falls continuously
E. Rises until the system "cuts off"

**Answer: C.** Rises until the system breaks down

**4. In a negative feedback control system, the controlled variable:**

A. Must have a set point
B. Need not have a set point
C. Is always anatomically direct from the controlled system
D. Is directly affected by external factors
E. Is located in the cervical nervous system

**Answer: A.** Must have a set point

**5. The controlled system changes its characteristics in response to demand of the controlled variable in which type of controlled system:**

A. Open loop
B. Negative feedback
C. Positive feedback
D. Adaptive
E. Feed forward

**Answer: D.** Adaptive
