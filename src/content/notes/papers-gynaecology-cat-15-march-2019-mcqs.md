## Multiple choice questions

### 1. Which of the following is a long-acting reversible contraceptive (LARC)?
- A. Levonorgestrel intrauterine system
- B. Vaginal patch
- C. Diaphragm
- D. Tubal ligation

**Answer: A.** LARCs are intrauterine devices and implants, and injectables are sometimes counted. The patch and diaphragm are short-acting, and tubal ligation is permanent.

### 2. Which one of the following is NOT a mechanism of action of Depo-Provera (DMPA)?
- A. Thickening of cervical mucus
- B. Inhibition of secretion of gonadotropins
- C. Prevention of follicular maturation
- D. Inhibition of ovulation

**Answer: B.** DMPA thickens the mucus, suppresses the LH surge (so ovulation is blocked), and thins the lining. It keeps a low basal FSH, which lets follicles grow without maturing, so it does not fully switch off gonadotropin secretion. The question is loosely worded.

### 3. Which of the following statements is false?
- A. The transformation zone is the area between the old and new squamocolumnar junction
- B. Carcinoma in situ is an abnormal change in cells but not invading deeper tissues
- C. Metaplasia is a change of normal epithelium to another type and is not reversible
- D. Neoplasia is the formation of new abnormal tissue that is not reversible

**Answer: C.** Metaplasia is a reversible adaptive change in the type of epithelium.

### 4. Which of the following is NOT a presentation of cervical cancer?
- A. Renal failure
- B. Back pain
- C. Intermenstrual bleeding
- D. Increased uterine fundal height

**Answer: D.** Cervical cancer causes bleeding (post-coital or intermenstrual), discharge, and in late disease back pain and renal failure from ureteric obstruction. The fundal height does not rise.

### 5. Regarding detection and diagnosis of cervical cancer, which statement is incorrect?
- A. HPV DNA screening is of value to detect high-risk individuals
- B. Visual inspection cannot detect suspected cervical cancer
- C. A Pap smear is recommended for all sexually active women above 30 years
- D. The malignant lesion starts at the squamocolumnar junction

**Answer: B.** Visual inspection with acetic acid and with the naked eye can detect cervical lesions and a growth. It is used in low-resource settings.

### 6. Choose the correct statement:
- A. Differentiated type of VIN most commonly occurs in young women
- B. Vulval intraepithelial neoplasia (VIN) can be treated by wide local surgical excision
- C. Topical treatment with imiquimod or 5-fluorouracil is ineffective against vulval intraepithelial neoplasia
- D. VIN 2 is considered low grade and usually goes away on its own

**Answer: B.** Wide local excision is a standard treatment, especially for differentiated VIN. Differentiated VIN affects older women with lichen sclerosus, imiquimod works for high-grade (usual type) VIN, and VIN 2 and 3 are high grade.

### 7. All of the following are supports of the uterus EXCEPT:
- A. Pubocervical ligament
- B. Uterosacral ligament
- C. Cardinal ligament
- D. Ligamentum arteriosum

**Answer: D.** The ligamentum arteriosum is a heart (fetal ductus) remnant, not a pelvic support.

### 8. An elderly woman comes to the hospital with difficulty in passing urine. Which is the best initial approach in her management?
- A. Prescribe antibiotics
- B. Refer her to a urologist
- C. Take a focused history
- D. Ask her for a urine sample

**Answer: C.** A focused history comes first, to find the cause (prolapse, drugs, neurology, infection) before tests or treatment.

### 9. The following are indications for the use of a vaginal pessary EXCEPT:
- A. As a temporary measure to allow treatment of underlying conditions
- B. Patients with ureterovaginal fistula
- C. During pregnancy
- D. Patients medically unfit for surgery

**Answer: B.** A pessary is used for prolapse (and stress incontinence) in women who are unfit for surgery or awaiting it, and during pregnancy and the puerperium. A fistula needs surgical repair and is not an indication. Option C is an accepted use, so B is the clear exception.

### 10. Choose the most appropriate statement regarding oestrogen replacement therapy:
- A. It causes ulceration of the vaginal wall
- B. Can cause carcinoma of the vaginal wall
- C. Can help improve a mild degree of vaginal prolapse
- D. Can cause dyspareunia

**Answer: C.** Oestrogen restores the thickness and tone of the vaginal tissues, and helps mild symptoms and pessary use. It relieves dyspareunia, and it does not ulcerate the vagina.

### 11. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of overflow incontinence?
- A. May be caused by an underactive or contractile detrusor muscle
- B. Can be associated with peripheral neuropathy
- C. Can be associated with a cystocele
- D. It is associated with the urodynamic finding of involuntary detrusor contractions

**Answer: D.** Involuntary detrusor contractions are the finding in urge incontinence (overactive bladder). Overflow comes from a distended bladder that cannot empty.

### 12. Regarding urinary tract infections, choose the most appropriate statement:
- A. Urethritis can present with pelvic pressure
- B. Pyelonephritis can be caused by a blood-borne bacterial infection
- C. Cystitis risk can be reduced by keeping the urinary catheter in place post-operatively for 3 days rather than one day
- D. Urethritis usually presents with back pain

**Answer: B.** Pyelonephritis usually ascends, but spread through the blood (for example Staphylococcus aureus) also occurs. Longer catheterisation raises the risk of infection, and back pain suggests the kidneys rather than the urethra.

### 13. A couple has infertility for two years. The WHO criteria for the minimum sperm concentration in normal semen is:
- A. 10 million
- B. 15 million
- C. 40 million
- D. 50 million

**Answer: B.** The WHO lower reference limit is 15 million sperm per ml (39 million in total).

### 14. Which of the following is most helpful in evaluating a woman's cause of infertility who has previous pelvic inflammatory disease?
- A. History about her siblings' offspring
- B. Pap smear
- C. Speculum examination
- D. Hysterosalpingogram

**Answer: D.** Tubal damage is the concern, and a hysterosalpingogram shows whether the tubes are open.

### 15. Using the Baden-Walker classification, a second degree uterine prolapse is characterised by:
- A. Descent up to the hymen
- B. Descent below the ischial spines
- C. Descent beyond the introitus
- D. Minimal descent

**Answer: A.** In the Baden-Walker system, grade 2 is descent to the hymen, grade 3 is beyond the hymen, and grade 4 is complete eversion (procidentia).

### 16. Which of the following is NOT a form of prolapse?
- A. Procidentia
- B. Rectocele
- C. Enterocele
- D. Urethral diverticulum

**Answer: D.** A urethral diverticulum is a pouch of the urethra. Procidentia is complete uterine prolapse.

### 17. In which of the following cases would the combined oral contraceptive pill NOT be prescribed?
- A. A patient with a history of 3 previous miscarriages
- B. A patient with a history of ectopic pregnancy
- C. A patient with a liver tumour
- D. A patient who is 35 years old

**Answer: C.** Liver tumours are a contraindication, because oestrogen can make them grow. The other three are not contraindications.

### 18. The following are risk factors for urinary incontinence EXCEPT:
- A. Diabetes
- B. Impaired cognition
- C. Nulliparity
- D. High-impact physical activities

**Answer: C.** Multiparity (not nulliparity) raises the risk.

### 19. A 23-year-old woman is concerned about her risk of cervical cancer. Which of the following will increase her risk?
- A. Her positive HPV screening with strains 6 and 11
- B. That she began smoking after high school
- C. That her mother was diagnosed with cervical cancer
- D. That she began sexual intercourse at 19 years

**Answer: B.** Smoking roughly doubles the risk. HPV 6 and 11 are low-risk types (types 16 and 18 are the high-risk ones), a family history adds only a small risk, and intercourse before 18 is the age associated with higher risk.

### 20. Which of the following are functions of the urinary bladder?
- A. Storage
- B. Emptying
- C. Filling
- D. All of the above

**Answer: D.** The bladder fills, stores and empties.

### 21. Regarding bladder innervation, which is correct?
- A. The sympathetic system is active during bladder emptying
- B. The parasympathetic system is active during bladder filling
- C. The pelvic nerve is involved in bladder emptying
- D. The hypogastric nerve is involved in bladder emptying

**Answer: C.** The pelvic nerve (parasympathetic, S2 to S4) contracts the detrusor to empty the bladder. The sympathetic hypogastric nerve works during filling.

### 22. Regarding gestational trophoblastic disease, choose the correct statement:
- A. Qualitative beta-hCG is used for monitoring
- B. Vitamin A deficiency is not a risk factor
- C. Hydatidiform mole can present with hyperthyroidism
- D. An invasive mole cannot progress to choriocarcinoma

**Answer: C.** Very high hCG stimulates the thyroid, causing hyperthyroidism. Monitoring uses quantitative hCG, vitamin A deficiency is a risk factor, and an invasive mole can progress.

### 23. Select the most accurate statement regarding choriocarcinoma:
- A. There is absence of chorionic villi
- B. It can only develop from a persistent hydatidiform mole
- C. Direct invasion of the myometrium is typically absent
- D. It originates from intermediate cytotrophoblast cells

**Answer: A.** Choriocarcinoma is made of malignant trophoblast without villi. It follows a mole, a miscarriage, an ectopic or a normal pregnancy, and it invades the myometrium and the blood vessels.

### 24. A 20-year-old has vaginal bleeding two weeks after treatment for a miscarriage. Which is she most likely to have?
- A. Incomplete evacuation of the uterus
- B. Hydatidiform mole
- C. Endometritis
- D. Asherman syndrome

**Answer: A.** Retained products of conception are the commonest cause of bleeding soon after a miscarriage. Endometritis also occurs, but with fever and pain.

### 25. The following are differential diagnoses of prolapse EXCEPT:
- A. Vaginal cyst
- B. Cervical polyp
- C. Elongation of the cervix
- D. Tumour of the ureter

**Answer: D.** A ureteric tumour is high in the retroperitoneum. A vaginal cyst, a prolapsed cervical polyp and an elongated cervix can look like a prolapse.

### 26. You suspect a woman with difficulty passing urine has a cystocele. Which of the following will you NOT do?
- A. Use a Sims speculum to aid examination
- B. Take a focused history
- C. Use a Cusco speculum to examine the patient
- D. Perform a cough test

**Answer: C.** A Cusco bivalve speculum holds up the anterior vaginal wall and hides the prolapse, so a Sims (single blade) speculum is used.

### 27. Regarding oral contraceptive pills, what qualifies as a very low dose oestrogen formulation?
- A. Equal to or below 15 micrograms of ethinyl estradiol
- B. Equal to or below 50 micrograms of ethinyl estradiol
- C. Equal to or below 35 micrograms of ethinyl estradiol
- D. Equal to or below 20 micrograms of ethinyl estradiol

**Answer: D.** Very low dose pills contain 20 micrograms or less. Low dose pills have 30 to 35 micrograms, and high dose pills have 50 micrograms. Some pills use 15 micrograms, so the question is loosely worded.

### 28. An endometrial sample report reads: "Endometrium has dilated glands with abundant endometrial stroma. The glands are of varied size but not overly crowded. The glandular epithelium is pseudostratified." Which diagnosis fits?
- A. Complex hyperplasia without atypia
- B. Simple hyperplasia with atypia
- C. Complex hyperplasia with atypia
- D. Simple hyperplasia without atypia

**Answer: D.** Simple means the glands are dilated and not crowded, with a lot of stroma. Pseudostratified cells with no atypia mean no atypia.

### 29. Which of the following is NOT a risk factor for endometrial carcinoma?
- A. Age above 50
- B. Levonorgestrel intrauterine system
- C. Obesity
- D. Unopposed oestrogen administration

**Answer: B.** The levonorgestrel IUS protects the endometrium. The others raise the risk.

### 30. A 48-year-old woman has abnormal vaginal bleeding with a foul-smelling discharge, and she worries about cancer. Which is she LEAST likely to have?
- A. Cervical cancer
- B. Endometrial cancer
- C. Vulval cancer
- D. Uterine fibroids

**Answer: D.** Bleeding with a foul discharge suggests a necrotic tumour. Fibroids cause heavy bleeding and pressure and rarely a foul discharge.

### 31. A couple has not conceived after 1 year of regular unprotected intercourse. Which of the contributory factors is inaccurate?
- A. Male factor 20 to 30%
- B. Both male and female 10 to 40%
- C. Female factor 60 to 70%
- D. Unexplained infertility 10 to 20%

**Answer: C.** A female cause alone accounts for about 35 to 50%, a male cause for about 25 to 30%, both together for about 20%, and unexplained for 10 to 20%. The figures vary by source, so the question is loosely worded.

### 32. The statements below refer to continence and incontinence. Determine which is true.
- A. Detrusor pressure and extra-abdominal pressure influence bladder pressure
- B. Urethral pressure is not influenced by the inherent urethral muscle tone
- C. Normally, coughing alters the pressure difference and leads to incontinence
- D. Overactive bladder results from uncontrolled increases in detrusor pressure and ensuing increase in bladder pressure beyond that of the urethra

**Answer: D.** The bladder pressure is the sum of the detrusor pressure and the intra-abdominal pressure (not extra-abdominal). Urethral tone keeps the urethral pressure high, and a normal cough raises the urethral pressure as much as the bladder pressure, so there is no leak.

### 33. Which of the following statements regarding the management of uterovaginal prolapse is incorrect?
- A. Can include physiotherapy and pelvic floor exercises
- B. Involves assessment of bladder function where a patient complains of urinary symptoms
- C. Exclusion of a pelvic mass which may be contributing to the prolapse
- D. Oestrogen replacement may be of benefit in premenopausal women

**Answer: D.** Local oestrogen helps postmenopausal women, whose tissues are thin from oestrogen lack. Premenopausal women make their own.

### 34. Benign conditions of the cervix: identify the false statement.
- A. When the columnar epithelium of the endocervix is visible as a red area around the os on the surface of the cervix, this is known as a cervical ectropion
- B. Ectropion is a cause of post-coital bleeding and may be treated by cryotherapy without smear testing
- C. Acute cervicitis may result from an STI
- D. Cervical polyps are most common in women over 40

**Answer: B.** A cervical smear (and exclusion of cancer) must come before cryotherapy for post-coital bleeding.

### 35. Regarding premalignant conditions of the cervix (cervical intraepithelial neoplasia, CIN), identify the false statement.
- A. CIN is the presence of atypical cells within the squamous epithelium, which are dyskaryotic with larger nuclei and frequent mitoses
- B. The histological finding in CIN III is that abnormal cells occupy the entire epithelium
- C. In CIN I, atypical cells are found only in the lower two thirds of the epithelium
- D. CIN can be considered a sexually transmitted disease

**Answer: C.** In CIN I the abnormal cells are confined to the lower third. CIN II involves the lower two thirds, and CIN III the full thickness. (Option B is printed as "CIN II" in the scan, which is read here as CIN III.) CIN is caused by HPV, a sexually transmitted infection.

### 36. Regarding progesterone-only methods of contraception, which statement is true?
- A. Failure rates of the POP are 4/100 woman-years
- B. Depo-Provera has a similar mode of action to that of the POP, but is less effective in preventing ovulation
- C. Use of Implanon is associated with a drop in bone density
- D. The POP makes cervical mucus hostile to sperm and in 50% of women inhibits ovulation too

**Answer: D.** The traditional pill works mainly on the mucus and also stops ovulation in about half of users. Depo-Provera is more effective at stopping ovulation, and the implant does not lower bone density.

### 38. Regarding emergency contraception, which statement is inaccurate?
- A. A single dose of 1.5 mg levonorgestrel may be taken, preferably within 24 hours and no later than 72 hours, after unprotected intercourse
- B. If beyond 72 hours since unprotected intercourse, insertion of an IUD usually prevents implantation
- C. The Mirena IUS can be used for emergency contraception
- D. None of the above

**Answer: C.** Only the copper IUD is used as emergency contraception (up to 5 days). The Mirena levonorgestrel IUS is not licensed for it.

### 39. Benign disorders of the vulva and vagina: which statement is NOT true?
- A. A typical presentation of lichen simplex is with itching and soreness of the labia majora
- B. Lichen planus can affect hair, nails and mucous membranes as well as the labia
- C. Vulval carcinoma can develop in 50% of cases of lichen sclerosus
- D. Vulvodynia is a diagnosis of exclusion

**Answer: C.** Vulval cancer develops in only about 3 to 5% of women with lichen sclerosus.

### 40. Which of the following investigations is best suited to determine whether or not a patient is ovulating?
- A. Temperature charts
- B. Mid-luteal phase serum progesterone
- C. Ultrasound follicular tracking
- D. FSH-based urine predictor kits

**Answer: B.** A mid-luteal progesterone (day 21 of a 28-day cycle, or 7 days before the expected period) above about 30 nmol/L confirms that ovulation happened.
