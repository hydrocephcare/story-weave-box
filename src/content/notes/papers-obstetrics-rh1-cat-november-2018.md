### Question 1. Describe 5 presumptive signs of pregnancy in the second trimester (5 marks)

Presumptive signs are symptoms and changes noticed by the woman, which suggest pregnancy but can have other causes.
1. **Amenorrhoea:** absent periods, the commonest early sign.
2. **Quickening:** the mother feels fetal movements from about 16 to 20 weeks.
3. **Breast changes:** enlargement, tenderness, darkening of the areola and prominent Montgomery tubercles.
4. **Skin changes:** linea nigra, chloasma (melasma) and striae gravidarum.
5. **Abdominal enlargement** from the growing uterus, and fatigue or urinary frequency.

Others: colostrum, nausea and vomiting (mostly first trimester), vascular spiders.

### Question 2. Describe 5 physiological changes in the gastrointestinal system in pregnancy (5 marks)

1. **Nausea and vomiting** from raised hCG and oestrogen, mainly in the first trimester.
2. **Reduced gastro-oesophageal sphincter tone** and a displaced stomach cause **heartburn and reflux** (progesterone relaxes smooth muscle).
3. **Slowed gastric emptying and intestinal transit** cause **constipation**, helped by increased water absorption, with **haemorrhoids** from straining and venous pressure.
4. **Gallbladder hypotonia** and more lithogenic bile raise the risk of **gallstones and cholestasis**.
5. **Liver:** alkaline phosphatase rises because of placental production, albumin falls, and transaminases stay normal.
6. **Gums** become hyperaemic and bleed easily (gingivitis), and cravings or ptyalism may occur.

### Question 3. Differentiate an incomplete abortion from a threatened abortion (5 marks)

| Feature | Threatened abortion | Incomplete abortion |
|---|---|---|
| Bleeding | Light or moderate | Heavy, sometimes with clots |
| Pain | Mild or absent | Colicky lower abdominal pain |
| Cervical os | **Closed** | **Open**, with tissue in the canal |
| Products of conception | None passed, still in utero | **Partially expelled**, some retained |
| Uterine size | Equal to dates | Smaller than dates |
| Fetus | Viable on ultrasound | Retained products on ultrasound |
| Management | Rest, reassurance, treat the cause, review | Evacuation (MVA or misoprostol), antibiotics, resuscitate if shocked |

### Question 4. Outline 4 areas of counselling during preconception care (5 marks)

1. **Nutrition and folic acid:** healthy weight, balanced diet, 400 micrograms folic acid daily from at least 1 month before conception.
2. **Infection screening:** HIV, syphilis, hepatitis B and malaria prevention, with immunisation (rubella, tetanus).
3. **Medical conditions and drugs:** control diabetes, hypertension, epilepsy, thyroid disease and asthma, and avoid teratogenic drugs.
4. **Lifestyle:** stop alcohol and smoking, avoid harmful exposures, manage stress and intimate partner violence.
5. **Family planning, birth spacing** and, where relevant, genetic counselling.

### Question 5. Outline the 6 pillars of safe motherhood (6 marks)

1. Family planning
2. Antenatal care
3. Clean and safe delivery by a skilled attendant
4. Emergency (essential) obstetric care
5. Postnatal care
6. Post-abortion care

### Question 6. Outline the components of a birth plan (4 marks)

1. Place of delivery and skilled attendant
2. Transport, including emergency transport
3. Funds and insurance saved for delivery
4. Birth companion, blood donor and care arrangements at home
5. Items to carry to the facility, and danger signs to act on

### Question 7. State 5 reasons why antenatal care is important for all women (5 marks)

1. **Detects and treats** conditions such as anaemia, hypertension, malaria, syphilis and HIV early.
2. **Identifies high-risk pregnancies** and plans delivery in a suitable facility.
3. **Preventive care:** tetanus toxoid, IPTp-SP, iron and folate, deworming and insecticide-treated nets.
4. **Prevents mother-to-child transmission** of HIV and syphilis.
5. **Health education and birth preparedness:** nutrition, danger signs, birth plan, breastfeeding and family planning.
6. **Monitors fetal growth and wellbeing** and gives psychosocial support.

### Question 8. Outline the components of comprehensive post-abortal care (5 marks)

1. **Emergency treatment** of complications: resuscitation, bleeding control, antibiotics, and evacuation of retained products (MVA or misoprostol).
2. **Contraceptive counselling and services**, because fertility returns within 2 weeks.
3. **Screening and treatment** for STIs and HIV, and Rhesus immunoglobulin for Rhesus-negative women.
4. **Linkage to other reproductive health services**, such as cervical screening and antenatal care.
5. **Community involvement and partnership,** and emotional support, including counselling for bereavement.
